THE VISION

Immortalizing Football Heritage

Legacy Eleven is a dedicated space for football lovers and elite collectors. We connect memorable events in sports history directly to physical collectibles, giving fans a premium, narrative-driven e-commerce experience.

The "Museum of Moments"

Instead of standard grid catalogs, our signature page allows users to stroll through football history—like Pele's 1958 World Cup debut or Maradona's 1986 "Hand of God" match—and shop exact replicas directly from the narrative.
