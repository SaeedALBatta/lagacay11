The Journey

Our Historical Timeline

1930

The Spark of World Cups

The inaugural FIFA World Cup in Uruguay lays the foundation for global football obsession. We began our archive here, cataloging the classic leather T-Model ball.

1986

Azteca & The Hand of God

Maradona's controversial brilliance in Mexico defines World Cup history. Legacy Eleven honors this era with authenticated Azteca ball replicas and signature stripes.

Legacy Eleven goes online, introducing the Museum of Moments page to give collectors a premium interactive portal connecting historic match stories to high-quality memorabilia.
