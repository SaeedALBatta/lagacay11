PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: June 07, 2026. This policy explains how we collect, store, and process your information when using the Legacy Eleven platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when creating an account, making a purchase, subscribing to our drops newsletter, or contacting us. This includes:

  • Personal identifiers: Full Name, email address, physical shipping address, phone number.
  • Transactional information: Cart items, checkout configurations, and purchase history.
  • Account credentials: Hashed passwords and session configurations.

2. Payment Security & Processing

Your payment safety is our highest priority. All transaction details are processed securely:

  • We do not store your credit card or billing details on our local database servers.
  • All checkout workflows utilize Stripe API tokenization and secure TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Transactions are fully compliant with PCI-DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard).

3. Cookies and Tracking

We use secure functional cookies to manage your shopping cart state, keep you signed in across page transitions, and store regional settings. You can manage or disable cookie configurations in your web browser preferences, though some functions like cart persistence may be restricted.

4. Your Rights and Data Control

Under relevant data protection laws (including GDPR), you retain full rights regarding your data. You may request information export, correction, or deletion of your account and personal details at any time by contacting our support team at support@legacyeleven.com.
